## 3. Spoken Voiceover Script (Exact Text)

> "The number one criticism you hear about wind turbines is that once they retire, they can't be recycled.
> 
> And while 90% of a turbine—the steel and copper—melts down easily, the massive composite blades and generator housings are virtually indestructible.
> 
> So one Swedish energy company decided to prove the critics wrong.
> 
> Instead of burying them, Vattenfall took a 10-meter nacelle—the giant pod at the top of a turbine—and turned it into a luxury tiny house. Think of it like a shipping container home, but it's already aerodynamic, hurricane-proof, and fully fitted with a kitchen, bathroom, and heat pump.
> 
> And the blades? They're extracting the carbon fibers to forge high-performance alpine skis, while other blades become architectural building facades.
> 
> Turning clean energy's biggest recycling challenge into homes and gear you can actually use."
