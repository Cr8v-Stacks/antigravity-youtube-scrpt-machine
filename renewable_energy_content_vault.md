## 🧠 The Curiosity & CTA Architecture (Why Standard Topics Fail)

Most renewable energy content on YouTube fails because it feels like **corporate ESG reports, environmental lectures, or dry tech specs**. To generate viral curiosity, high click-through rates (CTR), and explosive comment-section engagement (CTA), every topic must exploit **The Curiosity Triad**:

```
[Shocking Contradiction or Weird Number] + [High-Stakes System Obstacle] + [Contrarian Viewer Debate / CTA]
```

### High-Retention Packaging Rules:
1. **Numbers Must Be Concrete:** Use numerals and punchy units (e.g., `34.6%`, `18MW`, `5 km deep`, `100 hours`, `1913`, `$0.02/kWh`).
2. **Attack the Obvious Belief in First 5 Seconds:** Destroy the folk wisdom before explaining (e.g., *"Cheaper solar does not lower your electric bill—here is why."*).
3. **Engineered Comment CTAs:** End every video with a forced dilemma or binary choice rather than a generic *"like and subscribe"*.
